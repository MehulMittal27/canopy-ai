A ninth-grader builds a working radio and a helicopter from street scrap

A special encounter

“He builds all of this without instructions, relying solely on his own creativity - and using only recycled materials he finds on the street.”

Philipp, Burundikids, about a boy at the Birashoboka street children’s home. His radio works, with a large plastic canister as the amplifier. His helicopter’s rotor blades spin on a battery.
