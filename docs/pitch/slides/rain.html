Why now: this is Burundikids’ own update on the rainy season, so it is happening today, not in a case study. The post is about the Gateri refugee camp, where Burundikids runs a health station. Malaria and intestinal parasites were already among the most common diagnoses there in September. The medical team’s advice is the three things on the card: sleep under a mosquito net, remove standing water around the shelters, go to the health station straight away with a fever. The World Food Programme warning, as the post quotes it, is that more than a million people in Burundi are at risk between October and January, with El Niño raising the chance of floods and displacement.
