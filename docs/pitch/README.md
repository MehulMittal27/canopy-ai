# Canopy team pitch for engineers (version 2.1)

Source of the slide deck used to recruit engineers to Canopy. It was made as a Claude Slides artifact: `deck.json` holds the slide order, sections and fonts, and `slides/*.html` holds one file per slide (1920x1080 canvas, inline CSS, speaker notes in the `<aside>` of each slide).

Slide order: cover, origin, burundikids, challenge, rain, inventor, field, wow, workspace, platform, impact, areas, launch, join.

## Not included

Photos and the launch video are left out because they belong to Burundikids and show children. Slides that referenced them (`rain`, `inventor`, `field`, `launch`) still point to `/_blob/...` files that are not in this repo. Add them back only with Burundikids' permission.

## Notes

- The dashboard on the `wow` slide is an animated illustration with sample data, modelled on the real Canopy dashboard.
- Pupil and graduate numbers on the `burundikids` slide are 2017 figures.
- The `rain` slide quotes a Burundikids Instagram post; check it with them before sharing the deck widely.
